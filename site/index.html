PulmoGuard AI

An open research platform for low-dose CT (LDCT) lung cancer screening. It combines 3D imaging AI for nodule detection and characterisation with a language model for reports and an agent for follow-up scheduling.

開放的低劑量電腦斷層（LDCT）肺癌篩檢研究平台：結合 3D 影像 AI（結節偵測與惡性表徵）、大型語言模型（報告生成）與智慧代理（追蹤管理排程）。

What it does

系統架構

  • L4Clinician review workstation and PACS integration醫師審核工作台與 PACS 整合
  • L3Agents for follow-up, recall scheduling and report QAAgent：追蹤管理、召回排程、報告品管
  • L2Locally deployed language model with retrieval over screening guidelines本地部署語言模型 + 篩檢指引 RAG
  • L1Imaging AI: nodule detection (CADe), characterisation (CADx), segmentation影像 AI：結節偵測（CADe）、惡性表徵（CADx）、分割
  • L0DICOM ingestion and de-identification pipelineDICOM 匯入與去識別化管線

Results so far

目前成果

Pilot on the public LUNA16 dataset (subset 0, 89 scans, 112 nodules), using a pretrained MONAI 3D detector. CPM is the mean sensitivity at 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 1, 2, 4 and 8 false positives per scan.

於公開資料集 LUNA16（subset 0，89 例掃描、112 顆結節）以 MONAI 預訓練 3D 偵測器試跑。CPM 為每例 1/8、1/4、1/2、1、2、4、8 個假陽性時敏感度的平均。

Data

資料

Training and evaluation use public, de-identified research datasets from around the world, including LUNA16, LUNA25, LIDC-IDRI and NLST, each under its own licence. No patient data is published on this page.

訓練與評估使用全球公開、已去識別化的研究資料集（LUNA16、LUNA25、LIDC-IDRI、NLST 等），各依其授權使用。本頁不含任何病人資料。

Research use only. PulmoGuard AI is in research and development. Its outputs are not medical advice and it is not approved for clinical use.

僅供研究用途。本專案處於研究開發階段，所有模型輸出不構成醫療診斷建議，亦未取得臨床使用許可。
